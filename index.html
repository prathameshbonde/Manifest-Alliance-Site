    House rules

    Not before the show

    Don't apply upgrades before the snapshot. Applying them right before the event adds permanent power you can't reset.

    • ✕Governor Gear upgrades
    • ✕Charm upgrades
    • ✕Pet items

    Leave them in your backpack. Save them to unleash in your new kingdom during the next Kingdom of Power event.

    Post-credits scene

    Once you land

    When you're safely in your new kingdom, you get it all back right away:

    • Re-apply your saved Hero XP
    • Re-equip your hero gear
    • Put heroes back in your Drill Camp slots
    • Return to your full power

    Then hold your Governor Gear, Charm and Pet upgrades for the next Kingdom of Power event.

    Programme notes

    Transfer 101

    New to kingdom transfers? Here's how it works at a glance.

    What are Transfer Passes?

    The item you spend to move kingdoms. You need 1 to 50 depending on your Transfer Score. The in-game transfer screen shows your exact cost.

    Where do I get them?

    Claim 1 Transfer Pass a week from the Alliance Shop for 150,000 Alliance Tokens. Limit: 1 per week.

    How often do transfers open?

    During special Kingdom Transfer Events, usually every 6 to 8 weeks.

    Key requirement

    Your total power must be below the target kingdom's power cap when the snapshot locks.
